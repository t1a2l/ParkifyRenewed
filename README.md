# Parkify Renewed

**Parkify Renewed** combines the functionality of the original **Parkify** and **Campus Parks** mods for *Cities: Skylines*.

It lets you use park buildings as Parklife park modules, place parks in supported campus, industry, and airport areas, and optionally convert selected vanilla unique buildings into park modules.

> This is a renewed and merged continuation of BloodyPenguin’s Parkify and Campus Parks mods.

## Features

- Use vanilla and Workshop park buildings as Parklife park modules.
- Place Parklife parks on roads outside park areas.
- Place parks on pedestrian paths in campus areas.
- Place parks in industry and airport areas, provided the relevant DLC is installed.
- Optionally convert selected vanilla unique buildings into park modules.
- Fix missing boats on the Marina and Fishing Tours assets from *After Dark*.
- Improved placement and road-access handling for park buildings.
- Improved pedestrian-path snapping and external pedestrian-path connection support for park buildings.

## Requirements

### Required

- Cities: Skylines
- Harmony (Mod Dependency)

### DLC

- **Parklife** is required for Parklife park-module functionality.
- **Campus** is required for campus-area placement and campus pedestrian paths.
- **Industries** is required for industry-area placement.
- **Airports** is required for airport-area placement.

Campus, Industries, and Airports are optional DLC features. You do not need all of them to use the mod.

## Placement

Select a park building and place it in a supported area.

- In **Parklife park areas**, park buildings work as park modules.
- In **campus areas**, park buildings can be placed on pedestrian paths.
- In **industry** and **airport** areas, parks can be placed using the supported placement rules.

Shoreline park modules still require a road to be placed in advance when placed outside a park district, just like vanilla park modules.

## Pedestrian paths

Park buildings that contain pedestrian paths can be placed near external pedestrian paths. Parkify Renewed improves snapping and connection behavior so that the building’s internal paths are not incorrectly selected as connection targets.

Campus areas include pedestrian paths, making them the main non-Parklife area type where this feature applies. Industry and airport areas use roads rather than dedicated pedestrian-path networks.

## Unique building conversion

In the mod options, you can enable **Convert some vanilla unique buildings into park assets**.

This option is disabled by default. Converted unique buildings become parks and therefore lose the additional attractiveness boost provided by their original unique-building effect.

## Vanilla asset fixes

The mod can optionally fix two *After Dark* assets:

- **Marina:** adds the missing houseboat.
- **Fishing Tours:** adds the missing yacht.

These fixes can be enabled or disabled in the mod options.

## Compatibility

Parkify Renewed replaces the functionality of the original Parkify and Campus Parks mods.

Do **not** use the original Parkify or Campus Parks mods at the same time as Parkify Renewed. They may apply conflicting Harmony patches or duplicate placement behavior.

Enable Parkify Renewed in the Content Manager.
